About the Journal

When you return from travel and sit down for a cup of coffee with a friend, you’ll probably get asked something like, “How was your trip?” In practice, you don’t have all day to go blow by blow through your whole three-month itinerary. So, you tell them a few highlights, some things you saw and learned. It’s hard to be linear answering that question sometimes, so maybe you jump around as you recall your travels.

This is how the sketches are in this journal. Traveling itself can be an odd, unexpected road, and that is what I want to share.

While conversational in style, the sketches are also literary in approach. The site is laid out in a news print style, so the idea is you are reading each story as if you have a newspaper in hand. It will appear on screen as if it were on a page. This means no hyperlinks. I include a certain level of academic rigor, so if I think someone wouldn’t believe a claim or mention a certain piece, I add a citation in the Notes & Further Reading section below each story. But if I say this or that thing happened in this or that year, I won’t add a citation.

The sketches are narrative in spirit, not destination guides. Maybe they inspire the reader to want to go somewhere, but they wouldn’t provide practical advice to help them do it.

Some journeys down odd roads do not require a passport, train, plane, or vehicle to experience. They can happen with a walk down the street or in your living room. Some sketches include those stories too.

House Style

Form & Presentation

I aim for about 1,000 words per sketch, but the stories always run longer, sometimes way longer.

Each story should have between 1-3 photos. One at the top of the story to anchor the sketch, then an optional 1 or 2 to visualize other interesting parts.

Non-English words always in italics, but not for people or places. Example: I drank a caipirinha on Getuilo Vargas Street with a carioca in Pernambuco, Brazil.

Exceptions for the above can always be made for good, visceral writing.

All photos are taken by me or taken from an open source public commons.

Research & Accuracy

Exactly 0% of the sketches are written with AI tools. I may use AI as a glorified Google for fact checking and research, but any information presented in AI results are then found in an actual source outside of AI before being included in any story. But asking AI to write something, then putting that slop in a sketch does not happen here.

Given it’s a conversation, things I learn or read about could be incorrect or misunderstood. So, if something is factually incorrect, feel free to reach out to me at [email]. If a glaring mistake or several minor mistakes emerge, I may publish another updated version of that story. For full transparency, I will archive the previous version and make it available.

Editorial Judgment

Opinions will not be changed, no matter how strongly the reader’s objection. Here’s the difference between fact and opinion:

  • Guatemala City is the capital of Guatemala. That is a fact. If I write, Tegucigalpa is the capital of Guatemala, then this is a factual error that should be corrected.
  • Guatemala a City is the best/worst place to visit. This is an opinion. Those won’t be changed, no matter how strongly you disagree.

Travel writing is made up of observations from memory, research, and interpretations of the two. Guatemala City is a dangerous place is an interpretation that could be based on experiences and facts. There is some gray area for debate here.

Politics is important but I find people's unsolicited opinions on them boring. You will not find naked support of any political party, policy, or ideology in these sketches, though discussing them is possible.

People & Independence

I try not to publish names of people, especially if the story reflects negatively on them. Pseudonyms may therefore be used. I also try not to include brands or companies, unless it is very directly travel related. For example, I will say “rideshare” instead of Uber, but if I rent a car from a specific in-country company like Black Leopard 4x4 in Nairobi, Kenya, I may mention them.

This is not a platform to brag about romantic conquests. No kiss and tell.

I don’t write anything I wouldn’t say to someone’s face, or that I wouldn’t say in front of my Mom.

No company, guide, or product paid for a placement in any of these stories.
